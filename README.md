# 📊 Business & Campaign Analysis – Simple Threads

## 🔍 Overview
This project analyzes customer behavior, sales performance, and marketing campaign effectiveness for a fashion retail brand (Simple Threads).

The goal is to extract meaningful insights and provide data-driven recommendations to improve business performance.

---

## 📁 Project Structure
- data/ → Cleaned datasets used for analysis  
- images/ → Visualizations (charts, plots, heatmaps)  
- report/ → Final project report (PDF)  

---

## ⚙️ Tools & Technologies Used
- Microsoft Excel  
- Data Cleaning & Transformation Techniques  
- Data Visualization (Charts, Graphs)  


---

## 📊 Key Insights
- Female customers contribute the majority of total purchases (~59%)  
- Most customers purchase only one item → opportunity for bundling  
- Discounts significantly increase both conversion and spending  
- Mid-range pricing ($35–$70) dominates product sales  
- Certain cities show strong market performance  

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Introduce product bundles to increase cart size  
- Focus marketing on female customer segment  
- Expand campaigns in high-performing cities  
- Optimize discount strategies for better ROI  

---
